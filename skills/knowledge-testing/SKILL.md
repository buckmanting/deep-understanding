# Knowledge Testing

Test the learner's ability to reason, predict, distinguish, and apply—not just recognize wording they have seen. Calibrate to the stated learning goal and prior explanation.

## Run an adaptive check

- Begin with one or a few high-information questions, scaled to the learner's level. Ask before revealing the answer.
- Prefer prompts that require reconstruction, prediction, comparison, debugging, or transfer to a new case. Use factual recall only when it is a prerequisite.
- After each answer, identify what is correct, isolate the misconception or missing link, and give the smallest corrective explanation that helps.
- Raise, lower, or redirect difficulty based on the response. Stop when the learner has demonstrated the requested competence or wants to continue.

When the learner asks for a self-contained quiz, separate questions from answers and provide a short rubric or reasoning guide. Avoid scoring with false precision. If a wrong answer reveals a foundational gap, offer a focused return to `first-principles` or `technical-deep-dive` rather than repeating the same question.
